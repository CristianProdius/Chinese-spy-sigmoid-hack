## 1. Kilamba vs Kilamba Kiaxi (two different things)

- **Cidade/Centralidade do Kilamba** = Chinese-built (CITIC) planned new city, ~30 km south of central Luanda, started 2008, pop ~129k in 2019. Wikipedia lists it as Quilamba/Kilamba in Belas municipality, and warns against confusing it with "Quilamba Quiaxi". [SRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilamba)
- **Before 2024:** Law 17/11 created Belas municipality and says its seat is Cidade do Kilamba. [SRC](https://angolex.com/paginas/leis/lei-de-criacao-do-municipio-de-belas-na-provincia-de-luanda-17a-11a.html) (via search snippet). Decreto Presidencial 62/11 set up the Kilamba City administration inside Belas. [SRC](https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2011/decreto-presidencial-n-o-62-11-de-18-de-abril/)
- **Name overlap origin:** an old (2006) account says the Kilamba city project was originally called "Kilamba Kiaxi Social". [SRC](https://dw.angonet.org/wp-content/uploads/thomaz_ramalho.pdf) (search snippet). So a trace author mixing the two names has real-world cover.
- **Kilamba Kiaxi** is a separate, older Luanda municipality/district (inner-south/east, communes Golf and Nova Vida per the MPLA proposal text). [SRC](https://mpla.ao/2024/07/30/deputados-do-mpla-recebem-contributos-sobre-a-divisao-politico-administrativa-de-luanda/) (snippet). Spelling variants: Quilamba Quiaxi, Kilamba-Kiaxi, Quilamba Kiaxi. [SRC](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luanda_(prov%C3%ADncia))

### 2024 division (Lei 14/24 of 5 Sep 2024)
- Luanda province goes from 9 to **16 municipalities**. Icolo e Bengo leaves Luanda and becomes its own province. [SRC](https://angolex.com/paginas/leis/lei-da-divisao-politico-administrativa-14a-24a.html), [Ver Angola](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/082024/Politica/41061/Governo-não-revela-custos-da-nova-divisão-administrativa-por-processo-ser-gradual.htm)
- The law's Luanda chapter (Arts. 100-122) lists, in order: Ingombota, Cacuaco, Cazenga, Viana, Belas, **Kilamba Kiaxi** (Art. 109), Talatona, Mussulo, Sambizanga, Rangel, Maianga, Samba, Camama, Mulenvos, **Kilamba** (Art. 120), Hoji ya Henda. [SRC](https://angolex.com/paginas/leis/lei-da-divisao-politico-administrativa-14a-24a.html)
- **Key point:** under the 2024 law "Município do Kilamba" is its own municipality (Art. 120) and is distinct from "Município do Kilamba Kiaxi" (Art. 109). I could not read the article bodies, so I can't confirm which communes or neighborhoods each one holds, or whether Cidade do Kilamba maps 1:1 to Município do Kilamba. A puzzle written before or around the reform may still say "Kilamba, Belas".
- Sequele: "Município do Sequele" is in **Icolo e Bengo** province (Art. 132), not Luanda. An older government portal lists Sequele as a district of Cacuaco. [SRC](https://angolex.com/paginas/leis/lei-da-divisao-politico-administrativa-14a-24a.html), [governo.gov.ao](https://governo.gov.ao/provincia/luanda)
- Quenguela is a district in Belas, not a municipality. [SRC](https://governo.gov.ao/provincia/luanda)
- Zango: historically a Viana urban district (2016 reporting). [SRC](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/032016/DefesaSeguranca/3851/Luanda-pode-ganhar-mais-um-município-em-nova-divisão-administrativa.htm) Talatona was split off Belas into its own municipality.
- Camama: now its own municipality (Art. 118). Previously part of Belas/Kilamba Kiaxi area. [BG]
- "Urban districts" stopped existing under the new division. [SRC](https://angop.ao/en/noticias/politica/distritos-urbanos-deixam-de-existir-com-nova-dpa) A trace saying "distrito urbano do Kilamba" is pre-2024 wording.

## 2. Luanda water (EPAL-EP)

- **Utility:** EPAL-EP (Empresa Pública de Águas de Luanda). Regulator and ministry: MINEA.
- **Kilamba feed:** the **ETA do Kilamba** (Kilamba water treatment station) takes raw water through a conduit that passes the Bita area. Vandalism of that conduit in Bita forced the station to shut down in Feb 2025 and cut water to the centrality and nearby areas. [SRC](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022025/Energia/43255/Centralidade-do-Kilamba-sem-%C3%A1gua-pot%C3%A1vel-desde-Quinta-feira-devido-a-actos-de-vandalismo.htm) The notice did not name the underlying river source (Kwanza basin via Bita/Quilonga is my background assumption). [BG]
- A MINEA piece says the Kilamba ETA supplies ~60% of the southern and central areas; an O País list calls it one of the smaller plants. Figures conflict, so don't use capacity numbers as a clue. [SRC](https://mail.minea.gov.ao/index.php/component/content/article/19-destaque/610-ministro-da-energia-e-aguas-analisa-estado-e-melhorias-nas-infra-estruturas-de-producao-e-distribuicao-de-agua-em-luanda)
- **Other systems and projects:** Bita project (~71% in Sep 2026, covers Cacuaco, Talatona, Belas, Kilamba), Quilonga Grande, and Proágua (rehabilitates the Kilamba ETA, adds compact units in Kilamba and Zango). Start-up is now expected in early 2027. [SRC](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/092026/Ambiente/50111/João-Lourenço-acompanha-projecto-que-promete-mais-água-nas-torneiras-de-Luanda-no-início-de-2027.htm), [Ver Angola Proágua](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/072025/Energia/45178/Governo-lança-projecto-que-vai-fazer-chegar-água-potável-a-nove-milhões-de-pessoas.htm) Other plants and centres seen in the news: ETA Luanda Sudeste, Camama reservoir, Benfica II distribution centre (these serve other areas).
- **Hard to confirm:** I found no named "centro distribuidor do Kilamba" in public sources. If the trace names one, treat it as unverified, not as a decoy marker.

### Outage-notice wording (real examples)
- Nov 2026 (Camama/Benfica works): restrictions start "terça-feira, 3 de novembro de 2026", expected to last **20 dias**, no hours given. Quote: "a intervenção poderá provocar restrições no abastecimento em várias zonas", and asks customers to "armazenar água" and use it "de forma racional". Zones: Benfica, Patriota, Camama, Zona Verde, 10 de Dezembro etc. Kilamba **not** listed. [SRC](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/112026/Energia/50633/Algumas-zonas-de-Luanda-vão-ficar-sem-abastecimento-de-água-durante-20-dias-Saiba-quais.htm)
- Sep 2021: "o corte de água no período das 08h00 às 18h00" on a named date, plus "A EPAL compromete-se a repor o normal abastecimento o mais breve possível." Zones: Viana, Maianga, Ingombota, etc. [SRC](https://expansao.co.ao/angola/detalhe/epal-interrompe-fornecimento-de-agua-a-diversas-zonas-da-cidade-de-luanda--57239.html)
- Feb 2025 (Kilamba): "reparação do equipamento vandalizado, vai obrigar a paralisação da Estação de Tratamento de Água do Kilamba", with no restoration date. EPAL "veemente repúdio contra o acto de vandalismo". [SRC](https://www.verangola.net/va/pt/022025/Energia/43255/Centralidade-do-Kilamba-sem-%C3%A1gua-pot%C3%A1vel-desde-Quinta-feira-devido-a-actos-de-vandalismo.htm)
- Vocabulary seen or standard: corte, interrupção, restrições no abastecimento, paralisação, trabalhos de manutenção/melhoria, reposição / repor o normal abastecimento, restabelecimento, gradual (supply "gradualmente restabelecido"). [SRC] for restrições, corte, paralisação, repor; interrupção/restabelecimento/reposição as nouns are [BG] standard Portuguese.
- **How dates/times are stated:** weekday + day + month + year in words ("terça-feira, 3 de novembro"), time windows as "das 08h00 às 18h00" (24h, `HHhMM`), durations in days ("durante 20 dias"). Often **no end time or only "o mais breve possível"**. A notice with an exact end time is the rarer shape. [SRC] from the examples above.

## 3. Time

- Angola is **WAT = UTC+1, all year, no DST**. [BG] Luanda local 08h00 = 07:00Z. An ISO timestamp with `Z` or `+01:00` is consistent; `+00:00` for a local-time claim is an offset error.
- Date order: Angola uses **dd/mm/yyyy** [BG]. 03/11/2026 means 3 November in Angola but 11 March to US readers. Only a day > 12 disambiguates.
- Times are 24h in notices ("08h00", "18h00"). Watch an AM/PM or 12h-to-24h slip (e.g. "06:00 PM" read as 06:00).

- **Fortaleza de São Miguel** is the Luanda fortress on the bay, built 1576 and now the Museu das Forças Armadas, in the historic centre (Ingombota area). [BG] The brief's text uses it only to say Kilamba is *far from* the historic centre. **Pure flavor**, no sign it maps to an identifier.

I have no evidence either way since the input is unseen.
- The phrase "each clue is synthetic" tells us trace text is authored, not scraped from real EPAL notices. Real-world accuracy is a *weak* signal, but a planted wrong fact (wrong municipality, wrong plant) would be the very thing the author uses for the "bad" item.

## 5. Likely decoy patterns an author could build from these facts

1. **Wrong place:** a tool result about "Kilamba Kiaxi" (or Quilamba Quiaxi) outage fed to a Kilamba query. This is the leading candidate for the root-cause item.
2. **Right name, wrong era:** "Kilamba, município de Belas" (pre-2024) vs "Município do Kilamba" (post-2024). Probably a decoy only if the author cares about the reform; low weight.
3. **Neighbor zone notice:** Camama/Benfica/Talatona/Belas/Zango/Cacuaco outage returned for Kilamba. Plausible, and the Camama case is a real 2026 notice.
4. **Geographic nonsense:** Sequele or Zango treated as part of Kilamba.
5. **Stale or expired notice:** an old outage (e.g. the Feb 2025 vandalism shutdown) presented as current; compare the notice date to the run timestamp.
6. **Date ambiguity:** dd/mm vs mm/dd flips a window from past to future (03/11 vs 11/03).
7. **Timezone offset:** UTC vs WAT mixups (a 1-hour shift moves an 18h00 restoration across a boundary). Check `+01:00` vs `Z` carefully.
8. **Duration confusion:** "20 dias" vs an hours window; "restrições" (partial) reported as "corte total", or "restabelecido" vs "reposição gradual".
9. **Source-authority decoy:** a social-media-style or unofficial relay saying "EPAL" vs the real EPAL/ANGOP notice; or a different utility.
10. **Status inversion:** answer says "supply restored" while the notice says "paralisação" / "sem data de reposição".
11. **Plant mismatch:** notice about ETA Luanda Sudeste or the Camama reservoir vs the ETA do Kilamba feed.
12. **Id-level decoys (not domain, from the brief):** near-duplicate `SIGMOID_` ids by case, homoglyph, zero-width char, trailing space, `_V2`. Always compare the id **byte for byte** with the one referenced in the final model request.

- Grep the trace for "Kilamba", "Quiaxi"/"Kiaxi", "Camama", "Talatona", "Belas", "Zango", "Cacuaco".
